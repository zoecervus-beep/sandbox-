# Verification of research/frontier.json

Scope: adversarial check of the frontier researcher's regions, de jure / de facto records and events against the schema (`SCHEMA.md`) and against how `build/build.py` consumes them. `internal.json` was not touched.

Sources actually read: the five US State Dept International Boundary Studies (IBS 6, 25, 28, 164, 167; full text), the English texts of Gulistan and Turkmenchay (karabakh.org), the 1913 Constantinople Protocol (parstimes.com), Cordesman's CSIS chapters on the Iran-Iraq War, the Christian Science Monitor of 13 July 1988, Human Rights Watch's Anfal report, and Wikipedia pages (cross-check only; never sole source). Encyclopaedia Iranica pages answer 403 (Cloudflare) to the fetch tool, so Iranica content below comes from search-result excerpts that quote the article; where an excerpt did not carry the needed sentence I say so. Grokipedia was not used.

Rules adopted (needed to answer the consistency questions):
- **P1, presumed continuity inside modern Iran.** The renderer treats modern-Iran land as claimed unless a record says otherwise, and `build.py` carves every frontier parcel that touches modern Iran out of the core. A parcel inside Iran therefore needs complete records, and its claim is presumed from the 1800 baseline unless Tehran formally renounced it (Abbasabad 1828-1893, Kuhak 1871-72). Where the first documented Tehran act is later, confidence is lowered and the note says so (Sirri, Kuhak, Chabahar, Mirjaveh).
- **P2, documented-assertion rule for contested islands.** Where the island was held by another polity (Qasimi shaykhs of Sharjah/Ras al-Khaimah under British protection) the record starts at the first documented Tehran assertion: Abu Musa 1888, Tunbs 1903-04. Iran's present "always Iranian" position is retrospective and is recorded in the note, not mapped for 1800-1887/1903.

### B. Sirri, Abu Musa, Tunbs (answers the consistency question)
15. **f_sirri** de jure 1800: kept, `date_confidence` medium -> low, note rewritten. Tehran's first explicit act is 1887, but Sirri was a dependency of the Lengeh deputy governorship held for Persia by the Qasimi (c.1789-1887); the 1887 landing enforced an existing claim and Britain acquiesced (Aug 1888), so the claim is presumed from 1800 (P1). Source: Iranica "Abu Musa iii" and "Tonb Islands" (excerpts); IOR file "Sirri Island Occupation by the Persian Govt. 1887-1902" (qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100023650854.0x000030).
16. **f_sirri** de facto and **f_ev_sirri_1887**: split date 1887 -> 1887-09; event 1887 -> 1887-09, summary made precise (Feb 1887 "Persian Gulf Ports" reorganisation, Sept dissolution of the Qasimi governorship, garrison of 30 men and two cannon four days later). Source: Iranica "Abu Musa iii"; IOR file above.
17. **f_abu_musa** de jure 1888: start kept, note rewritten: first documented claim is the 1888 Persian military report (Iranica); the 1788-89 Persian arbitration over Farur/Sirri/Abu Musa is the earliest jurisdictional act but Iranica says the Persian and Sharjah claims "hinge on the interpretation" of the Qasimi confederacy of 1747-1887, so the retrospective claim is not mapped (P2). Source: Iranica "Abu Musa iii"; IOR Coll 30/163 (qdl.qa/en/archive/81055/vdc_100054941555.0x000091).
18. **f_abu_musa** de facto 1971-11-30 -> 1992-08-24: status `contested` KEPT; `date_confidence` high -> medium; note justifies the choice. `indirect` means a local ruler under the shah's acknowledged suzerainty, which the 1971 Iran-Sharjah MoU expressly avoided (both sovereignty claims kept; Iranian troops in a northern sector, Sharjah police and residents elsewhere); `direct` overstates Iranian control before 1992; `foreign_occupation` ignores the Iranian garrison. The vocabulary has no "shared administration", `contested` is the least misleading. Source: Iranica "Abu Musa iii"; Wikipedia "Iran-United Arab Emirates relations" (cross-check).
19. **f_abu_musa** de facto 1992-08-24 and 1888-1971 notes: 1992 note adds April 1992 (foreign workers expelled) as an alternative break date; 1888-1971 note says pre-1888 is not recorded because no Tehran claim is mapped. Source: Iranica "Abu Musa iii"; Gulf News "August 24, 1992".
20. **f_greater_tunb, f_lesser_tunb** de jure 1903: start kept (low), notes rewritten: documented acts are July 1903 (British-prompted Sharjah flag), March 1904 (Persian customs contingent lands, replaces the flags), 14 June 1904 (Persia withdraws "subject to the reservations"); Iranica also says the Lengeh Qasimi controlled the Tonbs c.1789-1887 and that the British refused in 1864, 1873, 1879, 1881 to endorse Sharjah's claims; no earlier Tehran statement found. Source: Iranica "Tonb Islands"; IOR Coll 30/163.
21. **f_ev_abumusa_1888** summary: "report ... for the British prime minister" -> "prepared by the Persian military for use by the prime minister" plus the July 1888 War Office map presented to the Shah. Why: Iranica does not say British. Source: Iranica "Abu Musa iii" (excerpt); The National (UAE) summary of IOR files (cross-check).
22. **f_ev_tunb_flags_1903** summary: "Belgian official, April 1904" -> sequence July 1903 / March 1904 / 14 June 1904. Source: Iranica "Tonb Islands".
23. **f_ev_abumusa_mou_1971** summary: MoU date given as 29 or 30 Nov 1971 (sources differ). Source: Iranica "Abu Musa iii"; Wikipedia (cross-check).

## 3. Still uncertain
- **Kotur polarity (1932).** Iranica says Turkey gained ~90 sq mi near Kotur; IBS 28 and Mojtahed-Zadeh (as quoted in Wikipedia) say Iran gained there. Iranica followed, confidence low; the Kotur strip location is a border-hugging illustration. Iranian-language scholarship gives ~800 km2 lost in total, which none of the English figures (60 + 90 sq mi) reproduce.
- **Zohab 'Transferred Territory' extent.** Only verbal descriptions exist (Naft-khana and the zone to the north; line leaves Naft-Mukataasy to Turkey). The ~330 km2 rectangle in Khanaqin district is a guess; Iranica's wording "around Qasr-e Shirin" is hard to reconcile with Qasr-e Shirin being in Iran today. Persia's 1934 challenge to the Protocol (renewed claim 1934-37) is not mapped.
- **Zohab lowlands** use the whole Khanaqin district as a proxy for the 1847 lowlands.
- **Firuzeh extent**: probably a Kopet Dag strip from Baba Durmaz to Lutfabad rather than a disc; the Hissar oasis (Persian gain in 1893) and the 'strip near Abbasabad' are not located; the Sarakhs (10 sq mi), Moghan, Dyman, Yedi Evlar, Atrek-delta and Namin changes of 1954-57 and the 1975 land adjustments (including Zain al-Qaws and Saif Saad) have events only because no coordinates or areas were found. Hashtadan and Musa Abad (MacLean 1891, Altay 1935) likewise.
- **Abbasabad fort position** uncertain by several km; surrender date 19 July (Russian) vs 22 July (Iranica).
- **Talysh/Mughan split 1813-1828**: treaty text supports the Mughan line and Zuvand in 1828, but the 1818 commission's Talysh line, the Astara district allocation and the date Russia retook the Lankaran coast (if it did) are unresolved.
- **Penjwin end date** (no source for 1984-87; 14 July 1988 is an inference) and whether Iranian forces held Haj Omran continuously (Cordesman dates Iran's first occupation to 1986, HRW/Wikipedia to 1983). Halabja withdrawal 12 vs 14 July. Faw's end 18 vs 20 April.
- **Herat 1801-1857**: `indirect` for 1801-1818 and 1833-1837 is an upper bound (Iranica vs Wikipedia "effectively independent"); status vocabulary for independent Sadozai/Barakzai principalities (`foreign_occupation` per the file's convention; `none` would be the literal reading).
