# Research archive

These files record the first research phase: `frontier.json` and `internal.json` from the two research passes
(format in `SCHEMA.md`), and `verify_frontier.md` / `verify_internal.md`, the fact-check changelogs.

They are no longer read by the build. `build/migrate.py` converted them once into `data-src/`, which is now
the source of truth. Later corrections live there; these files are kept as the audit trail of the research.
