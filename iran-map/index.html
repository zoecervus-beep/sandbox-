        How to read it

        Drag across the chart below the map to move through time, or use the arrow keys when the chart has focus. Shift with an arrow jumps to the next change. Click any area for its history in both layers.

        Before about 1880 most of these frontiers were zones, not surveyed lines. Areas are built from modern administrative units and carry a confidence rating, so treat their edges as approximate. Areas in km² are computed on an equal-area projection.
